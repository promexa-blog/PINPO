# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Small online sellers and their marketers making short social video ads for their own products. The first real user runs DemandMesh, an online shop in Kerala selling fashion, personal care, home and office goods, with group buys. They work alone at a desktop, sometimes check progress on a phone, and make ads in English, Malayalam and Hindi.

## Product Purpose

PINPO turns a product photo and a short brief into a finished 15–60 second vertical, square or landscape video ad. It plans a storyboard, generates AI scenes with fal.ai, animates the real product photo, adds the brand's own overlays, music, voiceover and outro, checks the result and exports a platform-ready MP4. Success is an ad the seller would actually post, made without an editor or a video team.

## Positioning

The product in the ad is the real product: hero and outro shots animate the original photo instead of letting AI redraw it, and every word on screen comes from the seller's own confirmed brief. Claims, prices and offers are checked before export.

## Operating Context

Runs locally on the seller's own computer (single user, 127.0.0.1). The seller pays fal.ai per generated scene with their own key, so cost estimates and mock mode matter. A typical session: pick a brand, upload a product photo, fill a short brief, build and tweak a storyboard, generate (minutes), review quality checks, download. Ads are kept in a library with versions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Multiple brand kits (logo or text wordmark, two colours, website, tagline, approved calls to action); every ad uses one brand revision.
- 8 templates, 4 outro types, 5 video models, 480p–1080p, 15/30/45/60 s, Instagram, YouTube and Facebook formats.
- Quality and compliance checks (claims, prices, rights, consent) gate export; some items need a person to review.
- No accounts, cloud storage or payments. No AI text generation (no LLM).

## Brand Commitments

The studio's own name is PINPO. Customer brand identity lives only in the ads and in brand kits, never in the studio chrome.

## Evidence on Hand

No testimonials, user counts or benchmarks exist; never invent them. The bundled example brand is DemandMesh (temporary low-resolution logo).

## Product Principles

- The real product is never altered by AI where it matters most.
- Nothing on screen is invented: no claims, prices or reviews the seller didn't confirm.
- Costs are honest: estimates are labelled as estimates.
- One clear next step at every stage of making an ad.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Usable with a keyboard and at 375 px. Malayalam and Hindi text must render correctly in the UI.
